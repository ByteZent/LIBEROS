> [!bluf]
> Social change is the change of a society in its **social structure**: power relations, values and norms, family structures, communication, organisations and institutions. It is usually **unplanned, unnoticed while it happens, and slow**.
>
> Two long trends describe modern societies. Life **differentiates** into separate spheres (politics, economy, law, science, the military), each with its own logic. And people move from small, personal **communities** into large, anonymous **society**. The armed forces are one of these spheres, and they recruit from a society that keeps changing around them.

## Definition

**Social change:** a change of society in its social structure, for example in
- power relations,
- values and norms,
- family structures,
- communication,
- organisations and institutions.

It is as a rule **unforeseeable**, **not conscious** and stretched **over a long period**.

Examples: the French Revolution, industrialisation, the civil rights movements, the collapse of the Soviet Union, digitalisation, perhaps artificial intelligence.

## Key Points

### Differentiation of spheres of life

- In a traditional society religion, law, economy, family and politics are bound up with each other.
- In a modern society they separate into spheres that follow their own rules, and new ones are added: science, art, education, media, health, the **military**.
- Consequence: no sphere can speak for the whole. Each sees the world through its own task.

### Community and society (Tönnies)

| | Community (*Gemeinschaft*) | Society (*Gesellschaft*) |
|---|---|---|
| **Relations** | Personal, "we-feeling" | Impersonal, anonymous |
| **Aim** | Cooperation, shared goal | Self-interest, different goals |
| **Form** | The original form of living together (family, rural life) | Relations defined by social roles, functional (modern, urban life) |
| **Make-up** | Homogeneous | Heterogeneous |
| **Held together by** | Trust, emotional ties, moral conviction | Laws and sanctions, rational means-end thinking |
| **Unity** | "Organic" | Constructed |
| **Examples** | Families, friendships, neighbourhoods, village life | The big city, the state with its institutions and organisations |

### Features of modern society

- speed of change,
- individualisation,
- economic dependence,
- globalisation and migration.

### Theories and types of society

| Theories of change | Types of society |
|---|---|
| Modernisation theories | Post-industrial society |
| Marxist theories | Risk society |
| Differentiation theories | Multicultural society, world society |

## Origins & Evolution

- **Tönnies** introduced the pair community and society as two basic forms of social ties [@tonnies1887gemeinschaft].
- **Durkheim** described the growing division of labour as the mark of modern society and asked what holds such a society together [@durkheim1893division].
- Later theory (differentiation theory) describes modern society as a set of specialised systems without a centre.

## Application

- **The military as a differentiated sphere.** The armed forces are analytically separated from society, and are understood as an organisation *within* social change. Their task and logic differ from those of the economy or science.
- **Science and the military follow different logics.** This is the tension of military science:

    | Science | Military |
    |---|---|
    | Methodical doubt | Certainty |
    | Debate | Uniformity |
    | Publicity | Confidentiality |
    | Networking | Closure |
    | Autonomy | Hierarchy |
    | Independence | Control |

- **A unit is a community inside a society.** Small units work through personal trust and a "we-feeling". The army as a whole works through rules, roles and sanctions. Good leadership builds the first inside the second.
- **A militia army feels change at once.** Because recruits come straight from civilian life, every shift in families, education, media use and values arrives in the barracks with the next intake (see [[Generations and the Military]]).

> [!counter] Critiques & Limits
> - **Romantic view of community.** Tönnies' contrast makes the village look warm and the city cold. Small communities also mean control and exclusion.
> - **Modernisation as a one-way street.** The picture of steady progress from traditional to modern fits Western Europe. Other societies took other paths, and change can run backwards.
> - **Description, not explanation.** "Differentiation" names what happened. It does not say why it happened here and then.
> - **Differentiation is not complete.** Politics, economy and the military remain entangled, as every defence budget shows.

## Self-Test: Social Change

> [!qard]- 1. Define social change and name three structures it can affect.
> A change of society in its social structure, for example power relations, values and norms, family structures.

> [!qard]- 2. Name three typical features of social change.
> Usually unforeseeable, not conscious, and over a long period.

> [!qard]- 3. Contrast community and society in three points.
> Personal versus anonymous. Shared goal versus self-interest. Trust and moral conviction versus laws, sanctions and means-end thinking.

> [!qard]- 4. What does differentiation of spheres of life mean?
> Areas that were once bound together (religion, law, economy, family, politics) become separate spheres with their own rules, and new ones arise (science, media, military).

> [!qard]- 5. Name the tension between science and the military in three pairs.
> Methodical doubt versus certainty. Publicity versus confidentiality. Autonomy versus hierarchy.

> [!qard]- 6. Apply: a rifle section and the army as a whole. Which of Tönnies' two types is each, and what follows for a leader?
> The section is a community: personal trust and a we-feeling. The army is a society: rules, roles and sanctions. Good leadership builds the first inside the second.

> [!qard]- 7. Limit: "differentiation explains why the military became a sphere of its own." What is wrong with this?
> Differentiation names what happened. It does not say why it happened here and then. It is description, not explanation, and the spheres stay entangled, as every defence budget shows.
