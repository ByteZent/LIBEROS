The core of the vault: atomic notes that each explain one idea in strategy, military affairs, public policy, economics, law, IR, psychology or sociology.

## Sections

- **Psychology & Sociology**: Perception, memory, judgement and motivation; values, norms and social change.
- **Strategic Theory**: Nature of war, strategy, deterrence, coercion, escalation.
- **Military Doctrine & Operations**: Operational art, doctrine, levels of war, joint and combined operations.
- **Public Policy & Governance**: Policy process, instruments, implementation, evaluation, institutions.
- **International Relations**: IR theories, order, power, institutions, norms.
- **Security & Defence Policy**: Defence planning, procurement, budgets, civil-military relations, neutrality.
- **Intelligence & Information**: Intelligence cycle, analysis, information and cognitive warfare.
- **Technology & Warfare**: Military innovation, cyber, space, AI, autonomy, emerging tech.
- **Economics**: Market failure, public finance, institutions, political economy, defence economics.
- **Law & Rule of Law**: Constitutional and administrative law, fundamental rights, international law, law of armed conflict.
