## How I take each proposal apart

The same seven questions for every proposal, following [[Polity, Policy, Politics]]:

1. **What changes?** The text itself: which article or law, what is added or removed. The proposal in one sentence, in my own words.
2. **Polity:** which instrument, which majority, who decided what before (Federal Council, Parliament, final vote). Is there a counter-proposal?
3. **Policy:** which problem is it meant to solve, by which mechanism, at what cost, for whom? What happens if it fails?
4. **Politics:** who is for and against (parties, associations, committees), which coalitions, which money, which arguments carry the campaign?
5. **The strongest argument on each side**, stated so that its own supporters would accept it.
6. **What voters need to know to decide**, and where they are likely to be wrong. This is the link to [[Do Swiss Voters Know What They Are Voting For]].
7. **My judgement** and what would change it.
