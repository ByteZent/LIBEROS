> [!bluf]
> The standard scholarly study of [[John Boyd]]. Osinga, a Dutch air force officer, reconstructs Boyd's work from his briefings and shows that the [[OODA Loop]] is a theory of **learning and adaptation** rooted in science and epistemology, not a slogan about speed. Read it to understand what Boyd actually argued.

## Summary

Osinga traces Boyd's intellectual sources (thermodynamics, Gödel, Heisenberg, evolutionary biology, systems and complexity theory, cognitive science) and argues that Boyd's strategic theory is about an organism's ability to keep adapting in a changing, uncertain environment. The OODA loop is the core of that argument, with *Orient* at its centre.

## Key Claims

- The popular "faster loop" reading is a gross simplification. Boyd's emphasis is orientation and adaptability.
- Boyd is a postmodern strategist: he draws on science to argue that no single model of war is final.

## Evaluation

The most rigorous treatment available: academic, and based on the full set of Boyd's briefings. It is dense; read the chapters on the OODA loop and "Patterns of Conflict" first.
