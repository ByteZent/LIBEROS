## Foundations of Political Science · HS26

**[[Autocratic State]]**

- Do autocracies decide faster and democracies better, as is often said? What would count as evidence?
- Which types of autocracy are most likely to start wars?
- The textbook's three kinds (monarchies, military regimes, civilian regimes) are now in. The rest of the note still has to be checked against ch. 3.

**[[Democratic State]]**

- Does direct democracy make security policy better or only slower? The fighter-aircraft votes would be a case.
- Is the democratic peace caused by democracy itself, or by wealth, trade and alliances that happen to go with it?

## Leadership I · HS26

**[[Attrition vs Manoeuvre Warfare]]**

- On a battlefield under constant drone and satellite surveillance, can operational surprise still exist, or does manoeuvre move into other domains (cyber, information, deep strike)?
- What would *unfreezing* require for a small defensive army like the Swiss one: its own innovation, or partners who bring it?

**[[Course of Action]]**

- How much of the COA process survives when decision cycles shrink to minutes (drone warfare, AI-supported targeting)?
- Is a militia staff better served by the full process (shared procedure) or by recognition-based shortcuts (which require experience it may lack)?

**[[Doctrine (Swiss Armed Forces)]]**

- Who in the Swiss system is supposed to drive doctrinal change, if Posen is right that armies rarely innovate on their own?
- Should a neutral state have a written *overall* strategy, and what would a doctrine at that level look like?

**[[General Model Theory]]**

- Where is the line between a *model* and a *theory*, if both are reductions made for a purpose?
- How do you notice, in practice, that a mental model has passed its pragmatic limits, before reality forces you to?

**[[OODA Loop]]**

- Can a *state's* policy process have an OODA loop in any meaningful sense, and what would "getting inside" it mean?
- Do AI-enabled targeting and decision-support systems speed up the whole loop, or only *Observe* and *Act* while *Orient* stays human?

**[[PMESII]]**

- Can PMESII capture *legitimacy*, which sits across the political, social and information sub-systems at once?
- How would a Swiss overall strategy use PMESII, given there is no single body that owns all six fields?

**[[VRIO]]**

- Can the *organisation* question be measured, or does it always end up as a qualitative judgement about doctrine and culture?
- In war the opponent can *counter* a capability without imitating it. Should CRIO have a fifth question for **counterability**?

## Microeconomics · HS26

**[[Circular Flow Diagram]]**

- How does a sudden surge in defence procurement travel through the circle, and which households' incomes rise first?

**[[Production Possibilities Frontier]]**

- How long does it take in practice to move along the frontier from civilian to defence production, and what limits the speed?
- Is European rearmament a movement along the PPF, a use of idle capacity, or does it require a shift of the frontier?

**[[Supply and Demand]]**

- How steep is short-run supply in defence industries, and what does that mean for how fast rearmament can happen?
- Where is the line between "prices as signals" in a disaster and exploitation, and who should draw it?

## Military Psychology · HS26

**[[Cognitive Warfare]]**

- Should the cognitive dimension be a separate operational domain, or is it a dimension of every domain?
- In Switzerland, who should own cognitive defence: the army, the Federal Chancellery, the cantons, or civil society?
- Does generative AI change the balance, or do people adapt their trust as quickly as the fakes improve?

## Proseminar I · HS26

**[[Bias in Research]]**

- Which of these biases apply to qualitative case studies and document analysis, and which need a different vocabulary?
- How do intelligence assessments deal with the same problems (source bias, mirror imaging)? Is a structured analytic technique a kind of pre-registration?
- Is there publication bias in military lessons-learned literature: are failed operations written up as often as successful ones?

## Not tied to a course

**[[CREW]]**

- How do intelligence assessments (key judgments, confidence levels) map onto CREW? Is a *confidence level* a qualifier, or a judgment about the evidence?
- When the disagreement is about the warrant, what counts as evidence *for* a warrant (Toulmin's *backing*) in strategy, where controlled tests are impossible?
