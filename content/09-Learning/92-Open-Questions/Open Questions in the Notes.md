## Foundations of Political Science · HS26

**[[Autocratic State]]**

- Do autocracies decide faster, as is often said? What would count as evidence? The lecture of 5 October answers only the other half: democracies solve problems better because they have stronger incentives to provide public goods.
- Which types of autocracy are most likely to start wars?
- The textbook's three kinds (monarchies, military regimes, civilian regimes) are now in. The rest of the note still has to be checked against ch. 3.

**[[Democratic State]]**

- Does direct democracy make security policy better or only slower? The fighter-aircraft votes would be a case.
- Is the democratic peace caused by democracy itself, or by wealth, trade and alliances that happen to go with it?

**[[Empirical-Analytical vs Hermeneutic Research]]**

- Is strategic theory (Clausewitz, [[John Boyd]]) hermeneutic, empirical-analytical, or something else?
- Can a normative question be answered scientifically at all, or only made more precise?
- How do mixed-methods designs combine the two currents without losing the strengths of either?

**[[Political System Model]]**

- How do direct-democratic instruments change the model? An initiative lets citizens articulate, aggregate and decide while bypassing parties and parliament.
- Where do social media platforms sit: as a channel for articulation, as an intermediary with its own steering power, or as part of the environment?
- How would the diagram look for an authoritarian state, where the input side is controlled from above?

**[[Polity, Policy, Politics]]**

- Where does "polity" sit in international politics, where there is no constitution and no central authority?
- Is military doctrine polity (a standing rule), policy (a content) or the product of politics? See [[Doctrine (Swiss Armed Forces)]].

**[[Sovereign Territorial State]]**

- Is the state system "atomising" into ever more and ever smaller states, or has the number reached a ceiling?
- Renationalisation: are states taking back competences from supranational organisations and markets under geopolitical rivalry?
- How much of the modern Swiss state goes back to the Napoleonic period (Helvetic Republic, Mediation, civil law)?

## Leadership I · HS26

**[[Attrition vs Manoeuvre Warfare]]**

- On a battlefield under constant drone and satellite surveillance, can operational surprise still exist, or does manoeuvre move into other domains (cyber, information, deep strike)?
- What would *unfreezing* require for a small defensive army like the Swiss one: its own innovation, or partners who bring it?

**[[Command, Leadership, Management]]**

- Do task needs and maintenance needs (or initiating structure and consideration) leave any room for a third aspect, or is Command simply part of the task side?
- Why does the white paper file the OODA loop and sense-making under Management and not under Command?
- Is there any empirical evidence that the three aspects are separate abilities, or do good leaders simply score high on all of them?
- How do the three kinds of sureness (*Handlungs-*, *Verhaltens-*, *Verfahrenssicherheit*) relate to the three kinds of reliability in the course plan? They look like the same triad.

**[[Course of Action]]**

- How much of the COA process survives when decision cycles shrink to minutes (drone warfare, AI-supported targeting)?
- Is a militia staff better served by the full process (shared procedure) or by recognition-based shortcuts (which require experience it may lack)?

**[[Doctrine (Swiss Armed Forces)]]**

- Who in the Swiss system is supposed to drive doctrinal change, if Posen is right that armies rarely innovate on their own?
- Should a neutral state have a written *overall* strategy, and what would a doctrine at that level look like?

**[[General Model Theory]]**

- Where is the line between a *model* and a *theory*, if both are reductions made for a purpose?
- How do you notice, in practice, that a mental model has passed its pragmatic limits, before reality forces you to?

**[[Mission Command]]**

- How much shared service does a militia unit need before mission command works, and can training make up for the lack of it?
- Does a common operational picture in real time strengthen mission command (a shared picture) or undermine it (control from above)?
- What exactly does FSO 17 say about deviating from a task? I have only the general definition so far.

**[[OODA Loop]]**

- Can a *state's* policy process have an OODA loop in any meaningful sense, and what would "getting inside" it mean?
- Do AI-enabled targeting and decision-support systems speed up the whole loop, or only *Observe* and *Act* while *Orient* stays human?

**[[PMESII]]**

- Can PMESII capture *legitimacy*, which sits across the political, social and information sub-systems at once?
- How would a Swiss overall strategy use PMESII, given there is no single body that owns all six fields?

**[[VRIO]]**

- Can the *organisation* question be measured, or does it always end up as a qualitative judgement about doctrine and culture?
- In war the opponent can *counter* a capability without imitating it. Should CRIO have a fifth question for **counterability**?

## Microeconomics · HS26

**[[Circular Flow Diagram]]**

- How does a sudden surge in defence procurement travel through the circle, and which households' incomes rise first?

**[[Comparative Advantage]]**

- How large is the efficiency loss that Switzerland accepts for security of supply (food, energy, armaments)?
- When does interdependence prevent conflict, and when does it become a weapon?

**[[Consumer Choice]]**

- Is military service pay better analysed as a wage (with income and substitution effect) or as compensation for a duty that leaves no choice of hours?
- How do rationing and price ceilings change the household's optimum, and who loses most?

**[[Elasticity]]**

- How inelastic is the short-run supply of ammunition and air defence missiles in Europe, and how many years until it becomes elastic?
- Which Swiss imports have the most inelastic demand, and what would it cost to make that demand more elastic (stocks, second suppliers)?

**[[Production Possibilities Frontier]]**

- How long does it take in practice to move along the frontier from civilian to defence production, and what limits the speed?
- Is European rearmament a movement along the PPF, a use of idle capacity, or does it require a shift of the frontier?

**[[Supply and Demand]]**

- How steep is short-run supply in defence industries, and what does that mean for how fast rearmament can happen?
- Where is the line between "prices as signals" in a disaster and exploitation, and who should draw it?

## Military Psychology · HS26

- Does the growing use of neuroscience in selection and training push military psychology towards a purely biological image of the soldier, and what gets lost?
- How far can conditioning go in combat training before it conflicts with the image of the soldier as a responsible, thinking agent that mission command requires?
