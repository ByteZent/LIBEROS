The course explains the experience and behaviour of people in training, leadership, selection and operations. Its method: observe first, then form several interpretations and test them, and apply more than one perspective to the same case.

## Assessment

| What | When | Form |
|---|---|---|
| Session exam | August 2027 | Written, 120 minutes, in the mother tongue. Covers HS 2026, the block week and FS 2027 including the intermezzi. Levels: know, understand, apply |
| Intermezzo: practice example | 11.10.2026 | Hand in a critical situation from military everyday life. Ungraded, required for admission |
| Intermezzo: crosscheck | 27.10.2026 | Mutual review of the practice examples |
| Intermezzo: short reflection | 16.11.2026 | Hand in the short reflection |

**Answer scheme:** observation – concept – mechanism – application – limit. Describe behaviour neutrally before explaining it.
