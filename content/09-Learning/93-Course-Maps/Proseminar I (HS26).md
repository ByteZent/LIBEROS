The proseminar leads from the critical reading of scientific texts to a small research paper of one's own. Quality comes from precise concepts, a justified research question, a traceable theory, testable expectations and open handling of sources and uncertainty.

## Assessment

| What | When | Form |
|---|---|---|
| Research question | 11.11.2026, 23:59 | Hand in and have it confirmed. Condition for the paper |
| Feedback | 10. and 17.12.2026 | Individual feedback sessions |
| Paper | 11.01.2027, 23:59 | Counts 100 %. 4–5 pages, at least ten scientific texts, PDF via Moodle with declaration of originality. Late without agreement: −0.5 grade points per day |

**Answer scheme:** question – theory – expectation – evidence – conclusion. Every claim is backed, every step of the mechanism justified.
