The course uses Great Britain from about 1780 to 1939 as a case for studying modernity and modernisation as conflict-ridden, uneven processes. Guiding question: in what sense did Britain become modern, and which political, social and imperial tensions did that transformation create? The aim is to understand historical debates, not to collect facts.

## Assessment

| What | When | Form |
|---|---|---|
| Source interpretation | 25.11.2026 | 1,200 words via Moodle: put the source in context, analyse it, judge it critically |
| Final exam | 16.12.2026 | Connect themes, concepts, processes and interpretations of the whole module |

**Answer scheme:** thesis – context – evidence – explanation – qualification. Chronology is the frame. The same change meant different things for different groups.
