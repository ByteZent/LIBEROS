The course makes decisions under scarcity analysable: how households, firms and state intervention are linked through incentives, prices and institutions. Problems are worked verbally, graphically and by calculation. Textbook: Mankiw/Taylor. The vault notes cite Mankiw's *Principles of Economics*, 9th edition [@mankiw2021principles], whose chapter numbers differ from the syllabus: elasticity is ch. 5 there, consumer choice ch. 21.
