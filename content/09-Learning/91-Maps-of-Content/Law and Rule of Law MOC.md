## How to Work with Legal Material

1. A **concept** note explains the principle (e.g. proportionality).
2. A **legal norm** note (`T - Legal Norm`) breaks one provision into its elements and interpretations.
3. A **judgment** note (`T - Judgment`) briefs a leading case: facts, question, holding, reasoning, significance.
4. Link all three. A graph cluster of concept + norm + cases is what you need for an exam or a legal essay.
