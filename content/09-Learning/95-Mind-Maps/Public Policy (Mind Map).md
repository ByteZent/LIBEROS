> [!bluf]
> What the vault holds on public policy so far, in one place. The first three branches follow the three facets of politics: the **polity** (the rules), **politics** (the process) and **policy** (the content). The next four are the disciplines that policy work borrows from: economics (why and how the state steps in), law (the form a policy takes), research methods (how to know whether a policy works) and sociology (who the policy is made for). The last two treat security and defence as a policy field and collect the material.
>
> Each leaf is a note of the vault, an open question taken from a note, or a gap. Filter by `todo` to see which notes are still missing, by `question` for the open questions, or by a discipline such as `Econ`.

## How to use this map

- One line per node, a few words each. Indent with four spaces to go one level deeper.
- Link freely: `[[Note]]`, `[[Note#Heading]]`, `[[/flashcards/|a page of the site]]`, `[text](https://…)`.
- A word in backticks is a tag and becomes a filter: `idea`, `question`, `source`, `todo`, or a discipline: `PolSci`, `Econ`, `Law`, `Methods`, `Soc`.
- A `todo` is a note that does not exist yet. Where a chapter of the textbook has only practice questions so far, the leaf links to them until the note is written.
- When a branch has grown into something I understand, write the note and replace the branch by a link to it.

## Key Connections
