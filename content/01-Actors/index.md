States, alliances and international organisations, armed forces and agencies, non-state actors, and leaders.

## Sections

- **States**: Sovereign states: strategic culture, interests, capabilities, policy posture.
- **Alliances & IOs**: NATO, EU, UN, OSCE and other alliances and international organisations.
- **Armed Forces & Agencies**: Militaries, ministries, intelligence services and policy bodies.
- **Non State Actors**: Armed groups, PMCs, corporations, NGOs and think tanks.
- **Leaders & Figures**: Decision-makers whose choices shape strategy and policy.
