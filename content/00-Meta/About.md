## Why this exists

Studying produces notes, but notes alone don't produce understanding. This vault is where course material, readings and current events are **distilled into ideas I can explain, connect and defend**. Publishing it forces clarity: a note that others can read has to stand on its own.

## Scope

- **Strategic theory & military affairs**: war, strategy, doctrine, operations, deterrence, technology
- **Public policy & governance**: policy process, instruments, security and defence policy
- **International relations**: theories, institutions, order, and actors
